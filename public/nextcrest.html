01 — The Problem

New leads were coming in — but nothing was following up

Nexcrest LLC is a real estate company that buys land directly from landowners — many of whom are confused, hesitant, or have been burned by bad experiences. These leads need education, trust, and consistent follow-up before they're ready to sell.

The problem? Every new lead that came into their RESimply CRM was just sitting there. There was no email follow-up system, no automated nurture sequence, and no way to stay in front of potential sellers without someone manually doing the work. Leads were going cold — and deals were being left on the table.

📭
No Follow-Up System

New leads entered RESimply but had no email sequence waiting for them. Zero automated nurture meant cold leads and missed opportunities.

🔌
No CRM-to-Email Integration

RESimply and Klaviyo were completely disconnected. There was no way for a new lead to automatically enter an email flow without manual effort.

🎨
No Brand-Consistent Emails

No on-brand email templates existed. Any communication going out was inconsistent and didn't represent the professional, trustworthy image Nexcrest needed to project.

🧠
No Strategy or Copywriting

Landowner leads need education before they sell — but there was no structured copy strategy addressing their common fears, objections, and questions about the process.

02 — The Solution

A complete lead nurture system, built from scratch

The entire system was built end-to-end — from a live CRM integration that fires the moment a lead is added, through to 12 carefully written and designed emails that guide a landowner from "who are you?" to "let's talk."

01
12 Custom Email Templates Designed in Figma

A full suite of 12 email templates was designed from scratch in Figma — matching Nexcrest's blue and orange brand identity with professional imagery, clear typographic hierarchy, and a layout designed to build trust. Every email was built for clean rendering across desktop and mobile, and structured to make importing into Klaviyo seamless.

Brand-Aligned DesignBlue + Orange Palette Mobile-FirstFigma Prototyping12-Template System
02
Email Copywriting — All 12 Emails

Every single email in the sequence was written from scratch — with a clear strategy behind each one. The copy was built to walk a landowner through a full trust journey: starting with a warm welcome, moving through education about land value, addressing real objections and title concerns, sharing social proof with real seller stories, explaining the process step-by-step, and closing with a clear, pressure-free call to action. Subject lines were crafted for open rates. Body copy was written in a conversational tone that sounds like a real person, not a robot.

Conversion CopywritingObjection Handling Social ProofEducation Sequence Subject Line OptimisationBrand Voice
03
Klaviyo Flow Setup with Strategic Timing

All 12 templates were imported into Klaviyo and built out as a fully automated nurture flow — "Resimpli Email Flow" — with strategic time delays between each email. Day 0 sends immediately. Subsequent emails space out over weeks, keeping Nexcrest top of mind without overwhelming the lead. The flow is live and processing incoming leads continuously.

04
RESimply → Klaviyo Integration via Make.com Webhooks

The most technically complex part of the project — and the piece that makes the whole system work on autopilot. A real-time integration was built using Make.com: when a new lead is added to RESimply (their CRM), a custom webhook fires instantly, passes the lead's data to Make.com, which then creates and adds that contact as a profile in the correct Klaviyo list. From there, the lead automatically enters the 12-email nurture flow — with zero manual data entry. No copy-pasting. No delays. No dropped leads.
