01 — The Challenge

A brand new business — nothing existed yet

Golden Kobe Family was a brand new pet business — named after their dogs Kobe and Moki — launching from absolute zero. No email templates. No automation flows. No copy. No strategy. Just a brand identity, a great product, and a growing audience that needed to be nurtured into loyal customers.

Without the right email infrastructure in place from launch day, they risked losing new subscribers immediately, abandoning carts without follow-up, and missing the chance to build a community around their brand before the competition got there first.

🆕
Launching Cold

The brand was brand new with no email marketing infrastructure at all — not a single flow, template, or piece of copy was in place.

🎨
No Brand Templates

There were no on-brand email designs. Every subscriber would have received generic, unconverted communications — if anything at all.

⚙️
No Automation

No welcome series. No cart recovery. No post-purchase flow. No win-back. Every revenue-critical touchpoint in the customer journey was completely missing.

📣
No Community Strategy

As a community-driven pet brand, building emotional connection with subscribers from the first email was critical — but there was no plan or copy to do it.

02 — The Solution

Built the entire email system from the ground up

The project covered every part of GKF's email marketing — design, copy, strategy, and technical setup. By the time the brand launched, they had a full, professional email machine already running.

01
Custom Email Design in Figma

All email templates were designed from scratch in Figma — built to match Golden Kobe Family's playful, community-first brand identity with their signature blue and yellow color palette, warm photography, and personality-driven layouts. The templates covered every flow category: welcome series, Bella emails, US and EU audience variants, survey list emails, abandoned cart, post-purchase, browse abandonment, win-back, and a special website launch email. All designs were created mobile-first and built for seamless import into Klaviyo.

Brand-Matched Design Blue + Yellow Palette Mobile-First Multi-Audience Variants 20+ Email Templates
02
Email Copywriting — Every Email, Every Flow

All copy for every email across all 5 flows was written from scratch — in a warm, playful, community-focused tone that feels like it's coming from real pet lovers (because it is). Subject lines were crafted to earn opens. Body copy was written to tell the GKF story, introduce Kobe and Moki, build emotional connection, handle objections naturally, and drive action at every stage — from first welcome to win-back re-engagement.

Conversion Copywriting Brand Storytelling Subject Line Optimisation Community Voice SMS Copy
03
5 Klaviyo Automation Flows — All Set to Live

All templates were imported into Klaviyo and built out as five fully automated flows — each with strategic timing, smart conditional splits, and multi-channel logic. Every trigger, delay, and branching condition was carefully configured so the right email reaches the right subscriber at the right time, automatically. All five flows went Live and have been processing subscribers since day one of the brand launch.

Every abandoned cart is chased — across both email and SMS. Every customer is followed up after purchase and invited to leave a review. And any lapsed customer gets a personalised 88-day win-back journey that runs completely on its own.

Starting Point

Built from absolute scratch

  • New subscribers get a 4-email welcome sequence that introduces the brand, tells the story of Kobe and Moki, and drives a first purchase — all automatically.
  • Abandoned carts are followed up with a 3-email + 2-SMS sequence, with a 10% off incentive, triggered within 1 hour of abandonment.
  • Every customer gets a shipping notification, delivery confirmation, and review request — on the right days, without anyone having to manually send anything.
  • Lapsed customers enter an 88-day win-back journey with an escalating series of warm, personalised emails — with a final non-opener branch for maximum coverage.
  • Browse abandonment is captured too — anyone who views a product and doesn't buy gets two timely, gentle reminder emails within 12 hours.
