01 — The Problem

A growing skincare brand with untapped email revenue

AuroraSkin had a quality product — an LED Facial Sculptor and premium skincare range that customers loved. But their Omnisend account wasn't doing the work it should have been. Emails went out inconsistently, the templates didn't match the brand's elegant aesthetic, and the automation flows that should have been recovering sales and nurturing customers simply weren't there.

Without a proper welcome sequence, abandoned cart recovery, or post-purchase flow, AuroraSkin was losing potential sales at every stage of the customer journey — every single day.

🔍
No Account Foundation

The Omnisend account lacked proper list segmentation, sending structure, and deliverability hygiene — limiting reach and engagement before the first email landed.

🎨
Off-Brand Email Design

Existing emails looked generic — not reflecting AuroraSkin's soft, feminine, premium identity. Poor visual design was undermining brand trust and click-throughs.

⚙️
Zero Automation Flows

No welcome series, abandoned cart recovery, order confirmation, or win-back flows. Thousands of potential revenue touch-points were completely missing.

✍️
Weak, Unstrategic Copy

Email copy lacked persuasion, urgency, and brand voice. Subject lines weren't optimised, and CTAs weren't pushing readers toward a purchase.

02 — The Solution

End-to-end email marketing built from scratch

Every piece of AuroraSkin's email marketing was rebuilt — starting from an honest audit of what existed, through to live, revenue-generating automation flows. The goal was a system that works 24/7 and feels unmistakably Aurora.

01
Omnisend Account Audit

Before touching a single email, a full review of the existing Omnisend setup was carried out — checking list health, segment structure, open/click benchmarks, deliverability signals, and existing campaign performance. This audit became the strategic roadmap for everything that followed.

List HygieneDeliverability Review Segment AnalysisCampaign Audit
02
Custom Email Design in Figma

A full suite of on-brand email templates was designed in Figma — featuring AuroraSkin's signature soft pink palette, floral accents, clean typography, and product-forward imagery. Every design was built mobile-first to ensure flawless rendering across all devices. Templates covered the welcome series, abandoned cart, order confirmation, and re-engagement flows.

Brand-Aligned DesignMobile-First Figma PrototypingMulti-Template System Pink / Floral Aesthetic
03
Figma-to-Omnisend Import

All designs were accurately exported and imported into Omnisend — built out as reusable template blocks that work across both automation flows and campaign emails. Pixel-perfect fidelity from design to inbox, every time.

Template ImportPixel-Perfect Build Reusable Block System
04
Email Copywriting & Strategy

Conversion-focused email copy was written for every flow and campaign — with a warm, confident brand voice tailored to AuroraSkin's audience of women seeking visible skincare results. Subject lines were optimised for curiosity and open rate. Body copy led with benefits, backed by social proof and urgency. A full content strategy was developed to keep the list engaged beyond the automations.

Subject Line OptimisationConversion Copywriting Social ProofUrgency & Scarcity Brand VoiceCTA Strategy
05
Automation Flow Setup

Four high-impact automation flows were built, configured, and activated in Omnisend — each mapped to a specific stage of the customer journey, timed strategically, and segmented for maximum relevance. These flows now run 24/7 without any manual input, converting subscribers and recovering revenue automatically.
