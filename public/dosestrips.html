01 — The Challenge

A unique product that needed unique emails

Dose Strips is a genuinely innovative product — but that novelty also creates a challenge. Most potential customers have never heard of dissolvable supplement strips. They don't know how they work, they don't know why they're better than the supplements they already use, and they need education and reassurance before they'll ever click "buy."

The emails needed to do more than just look good. They needed to explain the product clearly, build trust quickly, address the natural "but does it actually work?" scepticism, and move subscribers toward a purchase — all within a brand that felt premium, clean, and different from the typical supplement space.

❓
Unfamiliar Product Format

Dissolvable strips are still new to most consumers. The emails had to educate first — explaining what they are, why they absorb faster, and why that matters — before making any sales pitch.

💊
Heavy Supplement Competition

Shoppers already have supplement routines. The copy needed to show why Dose Strips are genuinely better — not just different — compared to the bottles already sitting on their shelves.

🎨
Brand Identity to Translate

Dose Strips has a bold, modern visual identity — deep reds, clean layouts, warm photography. Every email template had to feel unmistakably on-brand while still being clean and conversion-focused.

🛒
Cart Recovery for a Considered Purchase

Health supplement purchases are thought about. Abandoned cart emails couldn't just say "your cart is waiting" — they needed to reinforce trust, handle doubts, and create urgency through value, not just pressure.

02 — The Solution

8 emails designed and written to educate, convert, and recover

Two complete email series were built — a welcome sequence to nurture new subscribers and a four-part abandoned cart sequence to recover lost sales. Each email was designed in Figma to match Dose Strips' rich red brand identity, and every word of copy was written from scratch with a clear conversion goal.

1
Custom Email Design in Figma

All 8 email templates were designed from scratch in Figma — built around Dose Strips' deep red, blush pink, and warm cream palette. The layouts used bold typography, clean product imagery, and structured content sections that guide the reader's eye naturally toward the CTA. Designs were optimised for mobile rendering and built for clean import into Klaviyo.

Brand-Matched DesignRed + Blush Palette Mobile-FirstCTA-Driven Layouts Klaviyo-Ready
2
Welcome Series — 4 Emails to Build Trust and Drive First Purchase

The welcome series was written to take a brand-new subscriber from curious to converted. Email 1 delivers their discount code with a warm introduction to the brand. Email 2 explains the science behind why dose strips work better than traditional supplements. Email 3 tackles the two biggest customer needs — better sleep and better skin — showing how two strips solve both. Email 4 closes with real customer testimonials and a nudge to pick their bestseller. Every email carries a personalised discount code and ends with a specific, low-friction CTA.

Discount Code DeliveryProduct Education Two-Strip StrategySocial Proof Personalised CTAs
3
Abandoned Cart Series — 4 Emails to Recover Lost Sales

The abandoned cart sequence was built to work through four distinct angles — each one designed to meet the customer wherever they are in their hesitation. Email 1 is a warm, gentle reminder that their welcome code is still waiting. Email 2 shows exactly what they're leaving behind — product details, benefits summary, and proof that real people are seeing results. Email 3 reassures them with FAQs and makes the return-to-cart as frictionless as possible. Email 4 is the final last-chance push with urgency, customer reviews, and a clear "complete my order" CTA.
