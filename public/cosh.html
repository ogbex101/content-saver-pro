B2B Email Marketing — Selling to Businesses, Not Just Consumers
This project required a different approach to copy and design — speaking to procurement managers, site safety officers, and operations teams who need compliance first and price second.
01 — The Challenge

A 40-year industry leader with no email presence

Universal Containers had four decades of authority in the COSHH-compliant storage container industry — trusted by contractors, manufacturers, and public sector organisations across the UK. But that authority wasn't making it into their email marketing. The brand had no structured email templates, no welcome sequence, and no tested campaign strategy to reach the decision-makers who needed their products.

The challenge was multi-layered: COSHH compliance is a technical subject. The audience are professionals — site managers, procurement officers, HSE-conscious operations teams — who need accurate, credible information, not a consumer-style sales email. The copy and design had to be professional enough to land with a B2B audience while being persuasive enough to drive enquiries.

📋
No Email Strategy

There was no plan for what emails to send, which angles to test, or how to segment and target the different types of businesses that buy COSHH storage containers.

🎨
No On-Brand Templates

No email designs existed that matched Universal Containers' bold industrial brand identity — black, yellow, and authority-led — in a way that would resonate with a professional B2B audience.

✍️
Technical Copy Challenge

COSHH compliance, HSE regulations, and ISO standard containers require specialist copy that is accurate and credible — while still being compelling enough to generate leads and enquiries.

🤝
No Welcome Journey

New contacts had no onboarding email — no introduction to the brand's 40-year history, product range, or service offering. First impressions were being missed entirely.

02 — The Solution

4 templates. 3 campaign angles. 1 system.

A full email marketing setup was built for Universal Containers — strategy-led, copy-first, and designed to represent their 40-year brand authority at every touchpoint. The deliverables covered a welcome email and three A/B/C campaign variants, each testing a different conversion angle against the same core audience.

01
Email Marketing Strategy — B2B First

A B2B-specific email strategy was developed — defining the campaign structure, identifying the three most effective angles for converting COSHH storage buyers (safety/compliance, features, and discount/urgency), and planning an A/B/C variant approach that lets Universal Containers test which message resonates most with different segments of their audience. The strategy also covered the welcome email as a foundation piece for brand trust.

02
Copywriting — Technical, Credible, Persuasive

All copy was written from scratch — across all four email templates — in a tone that respects the professional B2B audience. Headlines led with the real business problem (hazardous chemical storage, HSE liability, site compliance) rather than generic sales language. Body copy referenced COSHH regulations, ISO standards, and HSE compliance accurately to establish credibility. Each variant had a distinct angle and CTA, designed to appeal to different buying motivations: safety-first, compliance-focused, or cost-conscious buyers.

03
Email Design in Figma — Industrial, Bold, On-Brand

All four email templates were designed in Figma — built around Universal Containers' black, neon yellow, and white brand identity. The design system is industrial and authoritative: bold condensed typography, high-contrast colour sections, product photography of their COSHH containers, and structured layouts with clear hierarchy that guides a busy B2B reader to the key information and CTA quickly. The welcome email went further — incorporating the brand story, 40-year history, service offering, how-it-works section, and customer reviews to build trust with new contacts.

04
Mailchimp Import + Delivery Setup

All designed templates were imported into Mailchimp and set up for campaign delivery — coded, tested for rendering accuracy across email clients, and ready to send. The three A/B/C variants were structured so Universal Containers can test them independently or as a split-test campaign to identify the best-performing angle for their audience.

03 — The Emails

3 angles tested. 1 welcome built.

Each of the three campaign variants approaches the same audience with a different conversion angle — giving Universal Containers the ability to test which message resonates best. The welcome email is a separate piece entirely, built to onboard and convert new contacts.

Variant A
Safe, Secure Storage for Hazardous Chemicals
Angle: Safety & Liability

Leads with the risk of non-compliant storage — staff safety, spills, and HSE liability. Positions COSHH containers as the responsible choice for any site handling hazardous substances.

  • HSE compliance positioning
  • Risk-first copywriting angle
  • Site safety decision-maker focused
  • CTA: "View COSHH Chemical Storage Containers"
Variant B
Protect Your Site With Compliant Chemical Storage
Angle: Features & Compliance

Leads with the product's key features — corrosion-resistant build, safe-purpose design, sump containment — and backs it with 40 years of contractor and manufacturer experience.

  • Product feature breakdown
  • Key features section with specs
  • Procurement & operations audience
  • CTA: "Get Your COSHH Container"
Variant C
Discount COSHH Chemical Storage, Ready to Go
Angle: Price & Urgency

Leads with a discount offer and "ready to go" availability — targeting cost-conscious buyers or those with an immediate compliance need. Showcases all three sizes (9ft, 10ft, 20ft) with pricing.

  • Discount badge + urgency framing
  • All 3 container sizes featured
  • Price-conscious buyer focused
  • CTA: "Get Your COSHH Container"
Welcome
More Than Just a Box
Angle: Brand Trust + Onboarding

A complete brand introduction email — 40-year history, what they offer, how they work, and real customer reviews. Builds trust with new contacts and positions Universal Containers as the go-to expert, not just a supplier.

  • 40-year brand story
  • "What we offer" service overview
  • "How we work" process section
  • Customer reviews / social proof
04 — The Results

A professional email system that matches the brand's authority

Universal Containers now has a complete, professional email marketing foundation — designed to communicate their authority, address real business pain points, and convert decision-makers into enquiries. The A/B/C variant approach means they can test and learn what drives the most engagement with their audience, while the welcome email ensures every new contact understands exactly who they are and why they're the best choice for COSHH storage.
