01 — The Problem

A growing agency with no transaction system.

OG Properties was handling a high volume of real estate transactions — condos, freehold listings, lease deals, buyer and seller closings — each needing its own checklist of tasks, lawyer coordination, document collection, and commission tracking. Managing all of this manually across a multi-agent team led to missed steps, disorganised closing folders, and no clear view of what was active, closing, or sold.

There was no way to automatically generate the right closing tasks for each property type, no pipeline view from pre-listing through to closed, and no system to alert agents when something was due.

Pain Points
  • No pipeline structure — deals had no stages from listing to close
  • Manual task lists — closing checklists were recreated from scratch every time
  • No commission tracking — Comm %, Gross Commission, # of Ends were unstructured
  • No deadline alerts — missed due dates on lawyer submissions and closing tasks
  • No historical data — 2022 and 2023 transactions had no accessible archive
What Was Needed
  • Full transaction CRM covering every property type and deal stage
  • Automated task generation — one trigger creates the full closing checklist
  • Smart pipeline movement — deals move boards when status changes
  • Commission columns — sold price, listing price, comm%, gross, ends all tracked
  • Alert system — 1-day deadline notifications for all outstanding tasks

03 — Automations Built

25 automations — zero manual pipeline movement.

Every deal stage change, task deadline, and document requirement is handled automatically. Key automations built:

⚡
When Property Type → Freehold Closing: automatically creates 20+ closing subitems (lawyer submissions, sign-backs, deposit tracking, and more) — instantly, with one status change.
🔔
1 day before subitem Due Date, only if Status is not Done → notify subitem Owner — no missed deadlines.
📦
When Contract Status = Closed → automatically move item to the Closed board.
🏠
When Contract Status = Sold → automatically move item to the Closing board for post-sale processing.
➕
+ 21 additional automations covering Condo Closing subitems, Lease Buyer tasks, board movement triggers, status cascades, and date-based notifications.
