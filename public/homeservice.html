01 — The Problem

Leads coming in. Nothing tracking them.

The business was receiving new job enquiries regularly — interior painting, deck repairs, cabinet refinishing, and more — but had no structured way to manage them. Leads were being noted in text messages or paper, callbacks were missed, and there was no way to see at a glance which jobs were hot, which needed a follow-up, or where revenue was coming from.

Commercial accounts — repeat business clients — had no database. Email outreach was ad hoc. And without a CRM, there was no way to track whether a lead had been contacted, what stage it was at, or what the next action needed to be.

Pain Points
  • No lead tracking — enquiries noted informally with no system
  • Missed follow-ups — no reminders or structured follow-up queue
  • No accounts database — commercial clients had no central record
  • No source tracking — couldn't see which channels were generating leads
  • No email system — outreach was unstructured and inconsistent
What Was Needed
  • Full lead pipeline — from new enquiry through follow-up to converted job
  • Service type tagging — each lead categorised by job type
  • Source attribution — track which channels drive the most leads
  • Accounts board — commercial client database with contact details
  • Email templates + mass tracking — outreach built into the CRM

03 — Pipeline Structure

New lead to converted job — every step visible.

The CRM was built with a clear 3-stage pipeline. Every lead moves through stages with status tags and notes, so the team always knows exactly what needs to happen next.

New Leads
  • Contact Attempted status
  • Appointment Set tag
  • Mobile, email logged
  • Project description noted
  • Est. hours tracked
  • Lead source tagged

Follow-Up
  • Move to Follow-Up status
  • Follow-up notes logged
  • Next contact date set
  • Previous conversation visible
  • Lead source carried forward
  • Est. hours sum tracked
03
Accounts
  • Commercial database
  • First + last name stored
  • Customer type: Commercial
  • Lead date recorded
  • Phone + email logged

Everything a home services business needs to grow.

CRM Boards & Views
  • Leads board — main table, input form, results views, dashboard
