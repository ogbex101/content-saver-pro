01 — The Problem

A streetwear brand with no email infrastructure

OTMCLO is a streetwear brand with a strong visual identity and a growing customer base — but no email marketing system to support it. Subscribers were joining the list and hearing nothing. Shoppers were abandoning carts with no follow-up. Customers were buying once and disappearing, with no automated touchpoints to bring them back or cross-sell into other collections.

The brand needed a complete email setup that matched its voice — direct, confident, Gen-Z streetwear coded — and an automation system that worked in the background to drive revenue without needing daily manual input.

📭
No Welcome Flow

New subscribers were added to the list and received nothing. No introduction, no incentive, no reason to make a first purchase.

🛒
No Cart Recovery

Abandoned checkouts weren't followed up on. Every dropped cart was revenue left behind with nothing being done to recover it.

📦
No Post-Purchase Journey

Customers bought and heard nothing — no order confirmation, no delivery update, no cross-sell to complete their fit or re-engage with the brand.

✍️
No Brand Voice in Email

The copy needed to sound like OTMCLO — not like a generic e-commerce template. Direct, casual, and culturally fluent for a streetwear audience.

02 — The Solution

Strategy → Copy → Design → Live

Every piece of OTMCLO's email marketing was handled end-to-end — starting with a strategic plan for which automations to build and why, through to live flows running in Klaviyo. The result is a system that captures, nurtures, recovers, and retains customers automatically.

01
Email Marketing Strategy

Before writing a single word or designing a single email, a strategy was developed — identifying the four highest-impact automation flows for a streetwear brand at this stage of growth, mapping the customer journey from subscriber to repeat buyer, and defining the copy direction. The goal was a system that felt natural and brand-authentic, not like a mass-market template.

Customer Journey Mapping Flow Prioritisation Conversion Strategy Brand Voice Direction
02
Copywriting — Every Email + SMS

All copy across all four flows was written from scratch — in OTMCLO's direct, no-fluff voice that resonates with a streetwear-native audience. Subject lines were written to stop the scroll. Body copy was kept tight and intentional. CTAs were written in the brand's own language — "your fit pulled up", "last chance twin", "one piece doesn't finish a look". SMS copy was written separately, optimised for brevity and immediate impact.

Brand Voice Copy Subject Line Writing SMS Copy CTA Optimisation Gen-Z Tone
03
Email Design in Figma

All email templates were designed in Figma — built around OTMCLO's black, white, and neon green brand identity. Layouts were bold, editorial, and streetwear-forward — using strong product photography, high-contrast sections, and a visual hierarchy that draws attention to the right thing at the right moment. Templates were built to be reusable across campaigns and optimised for Klaviyo import.

Brand-Matched Design Black / White / Neon Green Mobile-First Editorial Layout Reusable Templates
04
Klaviyo Import + Flow Setup

All designed templates were imported into Klaviyo and built out as four complete, live automation flows. Each flow was configured with precise timing, trigger logic, and conditional splits — including the SMS/email branching logic in the abandoned cart flow. All four flows were set to Live and are processing contacts automatically.
